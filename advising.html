Prospective students

I welcome inquiries from students interested in human-AI collaboration, open source communities, and computing education. Opportunities depend on research fit and available funding. Please email a brief description of your research interests and your CV. You may also include a link to a relevant project or paper. Mention your intended degree, institution, and start date.
